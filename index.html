Social media is a channel. A website is your home base.

Instagram and Facebook are great for reaching people. A website does a different job. Compare:

—Social media pageOwn website
OwnershipLives under the platform's rules, which can change or restrict your page.Your domain and your code. You decide what stays and how it looks.
SearchPosts are found mostly inside the app.Pages can be indexed by Google and other search engines for the services you offer.
StructureA feed sorted by date: key information gets buried under new posts.Services, terms, contacts and answers arranged the way clients look for them.
AccessSome content asks visitors to sign in or install the app.Opens in any browser on any device, no account needed.
LanguagesUsually one language per account.Separate language versions for clients abroad.
First impressionLooks like every other profile on the platform.A professional address for business cards, invoices and email.

A website does not replace your social media and we do not promise specific sales results. It gives your business an independent, professional presence that you control.

How we work

We may build your website before you even ask.

We look for real businesses that have an active presence but no website of their own. We study what they do and prepare a personal preview. You only pay if you want to keep it.

  1. We study your business

    Your public profiles, services, products and visual style.

  2. We build a personal preview

    Marked as a demo. It is a proposal from SpectrTech, not your official site.

  3. You request changes

    We implement agreed changes within the revision rounds of your package.

  4. Pay after approval

    Once you approve the final version, you pay and receive the site with its source code.

Unsold previews are deleted after 30 days. Prefer to start from scratch? Send us a brief. Start a project
