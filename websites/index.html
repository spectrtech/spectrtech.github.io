How a project runs

  1. Research

    We study your business, audience and visual style, or you send us a brief.

  2. Preview

    We build the site and share a preview link clearly marked as a demo.

  3. Revisions

    You send changes. We implement agreed revisions within the rounds of your package.

  4. Approval

    You confirm the final version.

  5. Payment

    Payment after final approval, before handover and launch.

  6. Handover

    You receive the clean site without demo marks, and its source code.

Questions

Do I have to pay before I see the website?

No. You see the site first. Payment is due after you approve the final version and before we hand over the source code and launch the site on your infrastructure.

Is the preview my official website?

No. A preview is a commercial proposal from SpectrTech, clearly marked as a demo. It becomes your site only after approval and payment.

Are domain and hosting included?

No, unless agreed separately. You pay the providers directly; the actual amount depends on the providers you choose.

Is there a subscription or mandatory support?

No. You pay once for the agreed work. Further changes can be discussed as a separate project.

Can I edit the website myself?

The site is delivered without a built-in editor. You receive clean source code that you or any developer can change.

Do you build multilingual websites?

Yes. Languages are agreed in the brief, and each version gets its own address and metadata.
