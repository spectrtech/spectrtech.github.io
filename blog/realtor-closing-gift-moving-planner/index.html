A Closing Gift Clients Actually Use: A Branded Moving Planner for Real Estate Agents

SpectrTech Guides · October 6, 2026

Closing gifts have a problem: they arrive at the one moment the client has the least attention to spare. A bottle of wine gets opened, a candle gets put on a shelf, and your name is gone with it. The gift that gets used is one that helps with what the client is actually doing that week — moving.

What makes a closing gift useful

  • It helps during the move, not after it.
  • It's used many times, not once.
  • It carries your contact details where the client — and anyone helping them — will see them.
  • It costs you little time to prepare per client.

A personalised moving planner

A moving planner covers the weeks around closing: a checklist from 8 weeks out, the address-change list, a moving budget and an inventory of every box. With printable box labels, your details travel on every box — on the truck, in the hallway, in the new home.

In the SpectrTech Moving Planner, you choose "I'm a real estate agent", enter your name, agency, phone, email and logo once, and create a copy for each client with their name, language and a short message. You send the client one HTML file by email or messenger. They open it in a browser — no account, no app — and every label they print says who prepared it for them.

Creating a client copy: the realtor’s name and contacts appear in the app and on every box label.

How to give it

  1. Send it early. The best moment is right after the offer is accepted, when the client starts planning the move — not at the key handover.
  2. Add a personal line. "Congratulations on your new home — here's something to make the move easier."
  3. Use their language. The planner works in nine languages; choose the client's when you create the copy.
  4. Follow up after moving day. A short message asking how unpacking went is a natural reason to stay in touch.

Privacy

Each client copy contains only your contact details and the message — none of your other clients and none of your own planning data. The client's boxes, addresses and budget stay in their own browser on their own device.
