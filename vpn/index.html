How it works

Three steps, no setup

You never type server addresses or keys. Everything comes in one link.

    Start @Spectrtechvpnbot in Telegram and choose a plan.

  2. Get your link

    The bot sends you a personal subscription link. It belongs to you only, so keep it private.

  3. Add it to an app

    Copy the link, open Happ, tap “+” and import from the clipboard. Then press connect.

Questions

How do I start?

Open @Spectrtechvpnbot in Telegram, press Start and choose a plan. After that the bot sends your personal subscription link.

Which apps can I use?

Happ, v2rayNG, INCY, sing-box and other apps that support subscription links. In Happ, copy the link, tap “+” and choose import from the clipboard.

How many devices can I connect?

It depends on the plan you choose. The bot shows the number of devices for each plan before you pay.

What happens when my plan ends?

The app stops receiving servers. Renew in the bot and the same link starts working again.

Who runs the servers?

SpectrTech VPN runs on the server infrastructure of a partner provider. SpectrTech runs the bot, the plans and support.

Are there rules of use?

Please use SpectrTech VPN in line with the laws of the country you are in. Do not share your personal link: anyone who has it can connect your plan’s devices.

How do I contact you?

Write to to@spectrtech.si and mention your Telegram username.
