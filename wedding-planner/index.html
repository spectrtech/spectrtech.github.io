What it is

A complete wedding planner that opens in your browser from a single file. No account, no internet needed — your data stays on your computer, with a one-click backup.

17 connected sections

  • Guest List — RSVP, meals, plus-ones, kids, tags; import from Excel/CSV
  • Seating Plan — drag guests onto chairs; Auto Arrange keeps families together and respects “keep apart”
  • Floor Plan — room to scale: tables, dance floor, stage, bar, photo booth
  • Budget — Estimated → Quoted → Final per line, category limits with overrun warnings
  • Vendors, Orders & Payments — compare quotes; choosing a vendor creates budget and payment entries
  • Wedding Day Timeline — hour by hour, flags overlaps and gaps
  • Invitations — 5 designs, personalised per guest, export PDF or ZIP
  • Dashboard, Checklist (30 tasks), Inspiration, Music, Photography shot list (27), Wedding Party, Honeymoon, Notes, Settings & Backup
