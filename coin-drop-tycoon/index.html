How it works

Drop. Merge. Multiply.

  1. 1
    $

    Drop

    Coins fall from the pipe and bounce through the pegs. Every peg a coin touches changes its value.

  2. 2
    +3+3›+4

    Merge

    Two equal numbers merge into a bigger one. Put your best number on the blue peg at the top: every coin hits it first.

  3. 3
    x1.5

    Multiply

    Upgrade orange pegs, unlock new ones and drop coins faster. Reach the stage goal and move on to the next board.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Optional rewarded videos give boosts and bonuses. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 and newer. There is no iPhone version yet.

Does it need internet?

Not for the game itself. Ads need a connection.

What data does it collect?

The game keeps your progress on your phone. Google AdMob collects some device data to show ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

When will it be on Google Play?

After the closed test. Join the test to play it first.
