Coin Drop Tycoon · Android game

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026 · App ID: com.spectrtech.coindrop

In short: the game has no accounts and we run no servers for it. Your progress stays on your phone. Google AdMob shows ads in the game (a banner, an occasional full-screen ad and optional rewarded videos) and collects some device data to do that, as described below.

Who we are

Coin Drop Tycoon is made by SpectrTech, an independent developer based in Baku, Azerbaijan. Questions about this policy: to@spectrtech.si.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (money, stage, upgrades, collected skins, sound and vibration choices) are saved in the app's local storage on your phone. We do not receive, upload or sell this data. Uninstalling the game, clearing its storage, or using Settings → Reset progress deletes it.

The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and it has no sign-in.

Ads by Google AdMob

The game shows a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad at most once every 10 minutes at a break in play (for example when you start a new stage or close a menu), and rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example to double a reward. All of them are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects the data below, encrypted in transit, and uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataExamples
IP addressUsed to estimate the general location of the device
Product interactionsApp launches, taps, video views
DiagnosticsPerformance data such as launch time and hang rate
Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Rewarded videos only play when you tap a button that offers one, and you can play without watching any. The banner and the occasional full-screen ad are shown automatically.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options inside the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The app itself only needs internet access to load ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google support libraries add network state, advertising ID and Android ad services permissions, plus background-task permissions (wake lock, job scheduling) that they use internally.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
