How to use this wedding checklist

Start at the block that matches how far away your wedding is and work down. Ticks are saved in your browser, so you can come back every week. Print it for the fridge. If your engagement is shorter than a year, compress the top blocks — the order stays the same, only the gaps shrink. The full reasoning behind each month is in the month-by-month planning guide.
