How to use the wedding budget calculator

  1. Enter your total budget and, if you know it, the guest count.
  2. Adjust the percentages to your priorities. Photography matters more to some couples than flowers — move 5% across and see what it does in money.
  3. When a real quote arrives, type the amount instead of the percentage. The remaining pot updates instantly.
  4. Download the CSV or print it for your meetings with vendors.

Typical wedding budget breakdown by percentage

Most planners quote a split close to this: venue and catering 40–50%, photography and video 10–12%, music and entertainment 5–10%, flowers and decor 8–10%, attire and beauty 7–9%, stationery 2–3%, rings 2–3%, cake 2%, transport 2–3%, favours and gifts 2%, and a 5–10% contingency you do not touch until the last month. Where you land depends on the guest count more than anything else: catering is the only line that grows with every chair.

Three mistakes account for most overspending: no contingency line, tracking one number per category instead of estimate → quote → final, and forgetting the small lines (tips, alterations, delivery fees, postage) that never appear on a quote but add up to a full category.
