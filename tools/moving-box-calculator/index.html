How the estimate works

Each room type has a starting number of small, medium, large and dish boxes. The calculator multiplies them by how many rooms you have and by how full your home is, then adds about 10% spare. Tape is estimated at one roll per 10 boxes and labels at two per box, one on the side and one on top, as recommended in how to label moving boxes.
