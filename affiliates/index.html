What is the SpectrTech affiliate program?

It is a free affiliate program for our digital products. You share your personal Gumroad affiliate link, and you earn 50% commission on every sale of the Wedding Planner or the Moving Planner made through that link.

Who can join the affiliate program?

Anyone with an audience of people planning a wedding or a move: wedding bloggers and planners, real estate agents, moving and home organizing creators, Pinterest, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube creators, newsletter writers and template sellers.

Is it free to join?

Yes. There is no fee and no minimum traffic. You need a free Gumroad account, because Gumroad tracks sales and pays affiliates.

How much commission do I earn?

50% of the sale price of each product you refer. For the Wedding Planner at $24.99 that is $12.49 per sale; for the Moving Planner at $29 it is $14.50 per sale.

How and when am I paid?

Gumroad pays your affiliate commission together with your regular Gumroad payouts, using the payout method set in your Gumroad account.

Where can I share my affiliate link?

On your blog, Pinterest pins, TikTok and Instagram bio, YouTube descriptions, newsletters, Facebook groups where self-promotion is allowed, or directly with your clients. Please do not spam or bid on our brand name in paid ads.

Do I need a website?

No. A social media profile, a newsletter or a client list is enough. Tell us where you plan to share the link when you apply.

Can I try the product first?

Yes. Approved affiliates get a free full copy. Ask for it after you apply. You can also open the free live demos of both planners on this site.

Why are these planners easy to recommend?

Each planner is a single offline file. No account, no subscription, the data stays on the buyer’s device, it works in 9 languages, and the buyer pays once.
