How it works

Step 1

Add boxes

Room, items, quantities, photos. Flags for Fragile, This side up, Open first, Heavy. Mark valuables "Hide on label".

Step 2

Print labels

A4 or Letter paper, or Avery sticker sheets. Big box number, room colour, flags and a QR code with the full list.

Step 3

Scan & find

Any phone camera reads the label. Or search every box from the planner: "chargers" → box 4, Office.

Questions

Do I need an app to scan the labels?

No. The built-in camera on iPhone and Android reads the QR code and shows the box list as text. It works without mobile data.

What if I change a box after printing?

The box goes back to "Not printed yet". Select that filter and reprint only those labels.

Can I keep valuables off the label?

Yes. Tick "Hide on label" on any item: it stays in your private list and search, but is not printed and not in the QR code.

Does it work on a phone?

The planner works in mobile browsers, but printing labels is easiest from a laptop or desktop.

Is there a subscription?

No. $29 once, and the file is yours.
